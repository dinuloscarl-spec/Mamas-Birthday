    Dear Mama,

    Happy 43rd birthday. We wanted to write you a real letter this time, not just a caption under a photo, because a few lines were never going to be enough.

    We don't say it often, but we notice everything you do. We notice that you're the first one up and the last one to rest. We notice that you ask if we've eaten before you ask about yourself. We notice the umbrella you hand us before we leave the house, and how you pretend you're only worried about the rain. It took us a long time to understand that all of that is you loving us in the way you know best.

    Growing up, we thought a lot of it was just what moms do. Now that we're older, we see how much of it was choices. You chose to stay up late so we could sleep. You chose to give us the bigger share, and to go without so we wouldn't have to. You chose to keep going on days when you were tired, and we never had to ask. Most of the time we didn't even know you were tired, because you hid it so well. We're sorry for the times we didn't notice, and we're thankful you kept doing it anyway.

    Thank you for showing up. You came to our school events, sat through the long programs, and waited for us afterward with that smile that made us feel like we'd done something great even when we hadn't. Being proud of us before we'd earned it gave us something to grow into. A lot of what we've done, we did because we wanted to make you proud, and we still do.

    Thank you for being honest with us. You told us the truth when we didn't want to hear it, and your advice was always a little annoying and almost always right. You taught us to be kind, to work hard, and to say sorry and mean it. We didn't always listen, but it stuck. When we see ourselves doing something good, we can usually trace it back to you.

    Thank you for how you forgive. We've said things we shouldn't have, and we've been stubborn and hard to deal with, and you still let us come back without making us feel small. You love without keeping score, and we don't think you know how rare that is.

    Thank you for the small things too, because they're the ones we'll remember most. The food you cook, the way you take care of your plants, the way you're always ready for a photo even when you're not in the mood, and how you make an ordinary day feel a little more special. Home feels like home because of you, wherever we are.

    We also want you to know we see how strong you are, even if you don't see it yourself. You carry a lot, and you carry it quietly. We hope you know that you don't have to do it alone, and that we're here to help now, the way you've always been there for us. We'll try to do better at slowing you down, making you rest, and taking things off your plate before you have to ask.

    On your birthday, we wish you rest, laughter, good health, and time for yourself, which you deserve more than anyone. We wish that the love you give away so easily comes back to you tenfold. And we hope that when you look at us, you feel that everything you gave up and worked for was worth it.

    We love you, Ma, more than this letter can say. Thank you for being our first home, our biggest cheerleader, and the reason we are who we are.

    Happy birthday.

    With all our love,
    Your kids
